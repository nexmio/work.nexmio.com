# Support

- **A bug:** [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/nexmio/work.nexmio.com/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). In the app, **Help → Report a bug…** opens the same form with your version filled in.
- **An idea or a missing feature:** [suggest it on GitHub](https://github.com/nexmio/work.nexmio.com/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml), or use **Help → Suggest a feature…** in the app.
- **How do I…?** The [start guide](docs/START-GUIDE.md) and the guide in the app (**Help → How to use Nexmio Work**) explain every part.
- **Privacy:** Nexmio Work sends nothing about you. Read the [privacy policy](https://work.nexmio.com/privacy.html).

Please do not paste private pages, passwords or keys in an issue: it is public.
