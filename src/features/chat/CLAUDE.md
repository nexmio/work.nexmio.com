# AI

Everything the AI does for pages: the chat ("Ask about my pages"), the fixed commands on selected text (`inlineAi`), in any text box (`fieldAi`), on a page, a folder, search, tasks and ideas, skills carried out page by page, proposals and their review (`proposals`), projects with AI instructions (`projects`). Rust: `src-tauri/src/ai/` (router, prompts, tools, agent, inline, proposals, providers), `chats/`, `skills/`, `projects/`.

- `ai::router::select_notes` is the only way to a provider. Pages go to a local AI, or to a cloud AI only after `ai.cloudConsent`, never in a Local AI only project.
- The AI is a box over the right side of the window (`AiBox`), not a tab of the right panel; it has two parts: **Chat** and **Queue**. Every job runs in the queue (`stores/aiJobStore.ts`), one at a time in the background, and the queue opens by itself when one is added. A command on selected text in a page is an `EditJob`: its result is accepted in the Queue, which opens the page and writes it (`findSpot` finds the text again; a changed text is never overwritten). A job whose answer is a list to tick (parts, steps, tasks, In short) is a `PickJob` (`addPick`, `PickJobCard`): the area that asks gives what to read, how to read the answer and how to add the ticked items. No AI job waits in a window. Every job has **Go to** back to where it was asked. Text boxes outside pages (`fieldAi`) and the search answer card still answer in place; they do not lock the screen.
- The AI never writes: every change is a proposal the person accepts (snapshot commit before, commit after).
- A command does one fixed thing and its output is only the result (no "Here is…"); clean it (`ai/clean.rs`) before it is shown.
- The chat is the assistant: besides pages it reads the projects, tasks and meetings and the help (`ai/assistant.rs`), offers places to open (`ChatEvent::Place`, `places.ts`, the same ids as `assistant::PLACES`), and may propose only tasks and parts (Edit proposals in the usual review). An area asks it a question with `askChat` (`src/lib/askChat.ts`); an area's AI button is the shared `AreaAiMenu`.
