# Ideas and capture

Ideas (lines in `Ideas.md` in the vault root, hidden from the tree, with a page indented under each) and the boxes that write something down: `CaptureBox` (one kind: New task or New idea), `CapturePicker` (+ Task, + Idea in meetings, Focus and the start page) and `QuickCapture` (the window; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+N asks T or I first). There is no quick page (removed after 0.27): a page is New → Page, and an idea becomes a page with To page.

- Say what, get only that: a box never asks again which kind it is.
- A task always goes into an epic the person chose (Into); Nexmio Work never makes an Inbox epic by itself. (The **Inbox** of the Pages panel is something else: the folders new pages land in.)
- The page being written is never changed by capturing.
