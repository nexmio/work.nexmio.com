## Add a picture

- **Paste** a screenshot or a copied picture (Ctrl/Cmd+V).
- **Drag** a picture file from your computer into the page.
- Click **Insert image** in the toolbar and choose one or more files.
- **Reuse a picture** that is already in the vault: open the **Images** tab of the right panel, click in the page where it should go, and press **Insert in page** under the picture. Or drag the picture from there into the page.

Nexmio Work copies the picture into the vault's **attachments folder** (usually `attachments/`) and puts a link in your page. A picture is never overwritten: a name that is taken gets `-1`, `-2` and so on.

## Look closer

Click a picture in a page, or in **Images**, to see it large. Press Esc to close. The large view also has **Open in another app** for pictures from Images.

## Size

Point at a picture in a page and a small bar appears: **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, **Original**. The size is written into the link, for example `![Chart|400](...)`.

## How pictures are written

Settings → Vault → **Insert images as**:

- **Markdown link** `![](../attachments/photo.png)` works in every Markdown editor.
- **Short name** `![[photo.png]]` is shorter and keeps working when you move the page, but only some other apps understand it.

Both are shown in the page in the same way.

## Images

The **Images** tab of the right panel shows every picture in the vault, which pages use it, and which are **not used anywhere**. **Move to trash** removes a picture after asking. It stays in the version history, so you can bring it back.

Pictures can be PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, SVG, BMP or AVIF, up to 25 MB.

## Where pictures are listed

Pictures are shown in **Images** and nowhere in the file tree, also the pictures that came with an imported folder. The files are still in their folders on disk and in the pages that show them. A folder that only holds pictures is not shown in the tree.
