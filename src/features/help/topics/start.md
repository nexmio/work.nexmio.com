## What Nexmio Work is

Nexmio Work is a pages app. Your pages are ordinary **Markdown files** (`.md`) in an ordinary folder. Nexmio Work shows and edits them, keeps every change in a built-in **version history**, and has an assistant, also called **Nexmio Work**, that helps you write and keep order.

## Vaults

A **vault** is the folder that holds your pages. When Nexmio Work starts, open a folder as a vault, or create a new one. New here? **Try the demo vault** on the welcome screen makes a small example (pages, an epic with tasks and two meetings) in a folder you choose, called `Nexmio Work Demo`, so you can look around before you use your own pages. You can have several vaults and switch between them (see _Several vaults_).

## The window

- **Left:** _Files_ (with a **calendar** for the daily pages and **Inbox** for new pages), _Search_, _Ideas_ and _Tasks_.
- **Middle:** the page you are writing. Open several pages as tabs.
- **Right:** a narrow row of icons: _Outline_, _Links_, _History_ and _Images_ open a small panel (press the icon again to close it); the speech bubble opens the chat about your pages (Ctrl/Cmd+L).
- **AI:** where you work, not in a column of its own: over selected text, the **AI** button above the page, right-click a folder, in search, tasks and ideas. See _Meet Nexmio Work_.
- **Bottom:** the save status, the project, the AI in use and the version of the app.

## Good to know

- You never press "save". Pages are saved a moment after you stop typing, and committed to the version history. Nothing is lost.
- Pause the mouse over a button to see what it does. A small **?** next to a setting explains it.
- Everything in this guide can be reached from the **?** button in the top bar, from **Help → How to use Nexmio Work**, or by pressing **F1**.

## Your own order

The **Sort** button above the file tree has **My own order**. Then nothing is sorted for you. Drag a page or folder to the top edge or bottom edge of another to place it before or after it, or right-click and choose **Move up / Move down** (also **Alt+Up / Alt+Down**). The middle of a folder still moves the item into the folder. The order is saved in the vault, for All files and for each project, and new pages are added last. The other sort choices, Name and Last changed, never change your order.

## Select several files

**Ctrl/Cmd+click** items in the file tree to tick them, or click one and **Shift+click** another to tick everything between. Then right-click a ticked item and choose **Move N items to…** or **Delete N items**, or drag them together onto a folder or a project. A plain click clears the selection. Deleted pages can be brought back with **File → Recover deleted pages**.

**Tags on a whole folder.** Right-click a folder or a project and choose **Tags › Add a tag to all pages…** (or **Remove a tag from all pages…**). Type a tag (the ones you already use are offered), and the window says how many pages in the folder and its folders will change. When you press the button, the tag is written in the properties of each of those pages (so any editor sees it), and only pages that change are touched. A snapshot is saved before and after, so History can bring every page back. Pages added to the folder later do not get the tag, and the folder itself has no tag: nothing is inherited.

## Recent, Projects and All files

The file panel has three parts. **Recent** lists the pages you opened last (4 by default; change it in **Settings → Pages and backup → Recent pages shown**, 0 hides it). **Projects** shows every project with its own pages. **All files** holds everything that is not in a project or in the Inbox.

## Found a bug, or have an idea?

Use **Help → Report a bug…** or **Help → Suggest a feature…**. The same menu has **Report inappropriate AI content…** for an AI answer that was wrong or harmful. Your browser opens a page on GitHub with the form already started (Nexmio Work version and system filled in). You need a free GitHub account to send it. Do not paste private pages.

## Inbox and daily pages

- **Inbox** in the Files panel holds the folders new pages land in by themselves: Meetings, Tasks and the pages made from ideas. New pages have a **NEW** mark for a week until you open them. Use the button, or right-click → **Move to…**, to move a page to a project or a folder when it has a home.
- **Daily pages** are a calendar at the top of the Files panel, with a dot on every day that has a page. Click a day to open its page, or to make it. **Today** opens today's page (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+D does too). The daily pages folder is not listed in All files: the calendar is the way to them.

## The start page

**Get started** stands at the top of the start page in a vault that is new to Nexmio Work: five small steps that show how it fits together (make a plan, break it into parts, make a task of a part, start a timer on a task, write a meeting). Each step ticks itself when your pages show it is done, and the next one has the main button. The card goes away when all five are done; **Close** hides it for this vault.

The page is made of widgets you can change: press **Customize**, then **Add widget**, move a widget with its arrows, choose **Half** or **Full** width, or remove it with the cross. **Reset** goes back to the page you start with: **Today** (the next meeting, what you are doing and waiting for, and where you left off), **Pinned**, **Recent**, **Epic progress**, **Plans** and **This week** (what you finished, the time logged and the meetings this week, with **Look back**), in up to three columns. A layout you have already saved is kept. The layout is kept on this computer. **Start** at the top of the navigation opens the start page. It also shows when no page is open. **Continue** is the page you worked on last, with its first lines; **Recent** lists the pages you opened lately (the filters show only pages, meetings or epics); **Pinned** lists the pages, epics and tasks you pinned: right-click a page (in the file list or on its tab), an epic or a task and choose **Pin**, or use the **···** menu above a page or an epic. Everything comes from your own pages, so it works without AI. To open Nexmio Work on the start page instead of the last page, tick **Open the start page when Nexmio Work starts** in Settings → Appearance.
