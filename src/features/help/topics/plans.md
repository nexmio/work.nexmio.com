## Plans: break something big down

A big thing is hard to start. Writing it down as parts, and the parts as smaller parts, makes it smaller. A **plan** is where you do that. It is an ordinary page (with the tag `plan`, kept in the folder `Plans`) whose list of parts is a tree, so you can open it in any other editor too. There is no timer and no deadline: a plan goes on for as long as you like.

### Plans and projects

A **project** is a folder with its own AI rules (see Projects). When you make a plan, the box **Make a project for it** makes one: a folder with the name of the plan, with the plan in it. The epics and pages you make from the plan are kept inside it, in the folders `Epics` and `Notes`, so everything about the plan is in one place. Without the box, the plan is kept in the folder Plans.

A project that is already there can be made a plan: press **Plan from a project…** on the page you see with no plan open, or right-click the project in Files and choose **Make a plan of this project**. Nexmio Work adds a plan page to the project's folder and can start it with the project's pages and epics as parts, linked. Nothing in the project is moved or changed, and a project never becomes a plan unless you ask.

### Make a plan

Press **New plan** at the top of the navigation, or **New** at the top of the plans list. With no plan open, the page offers **Empty plan** and **Plan from a project**. **Break it down** takes you through three short steps, and everything is saved as you go, so you can stop and come back:

1. **What is it?** Write what you want to get done.
2. **What are the parts?** Write the parts as they come, and press **Break it down** on a part to write smaller ones under it. **Suggest parts** lets the AI propose parts; you tick the ones you want.
3. **Where to start.** Choose the one smallest thing to begin with, and make it a task now, or leave it for later.

### The outline and the map

**Outline** shows the parts as an indented list you can fold. Click a part to choose it, double-click to change its words, and press **···** for more. **Map** shows the plan as a box at the top, and the parts below it as boxes with lines. Choose a box and its actions are in the **Part** tab of the right panel (grouped as Parts, Link to, Arrange the parts and Move, with Repeat and Delete at the bottom), or right-click it for the same menu; double-click it to change its words, or press the pencil in the panel. They are the same list: a change in one shows in the other. Under the outline, **A small place to start** points at the first open task.

### Write it all down first

In the outline, the field **+ Add a part** takes parts one after the other: **Enter** adds one and stays, **Tab** makes the next one a smaller part of the one you just wrote, and **Shift+Tab** goes back up a level. You can also paste a list (one part on each line, indented for smaller parts) and it becomes a tree at once. Nothing has to be tied to anything yet: come back later and tie only the parts you want to.

### Tie a part to the rest of Nexmio Work

Press **···** on a part:

- **Break it down…** asks the AI for parts of the part (you tick which to add).
- **Add a part** writes a smaller part under it.
- **Link or make an epic…** links an epic that is already there, or makes a new one: its name is the part's, and the parts you tick become its tasks. The part shows **Epic · 2 of 5**, which opens it.
- **Link or make a task…** links a task that is already there, or makes a new one in the epic of the plan (an epic with the plan's name, made if it is not there). The part shows **Task**.
- **Link or make a page…** links a page, or makes a new one with a link back to the plan. The part shows **Page · name**.
- **Repeat these steps for…** is for things you do the same way more than once, such as asking several suppliers the same questions. Write the steps as parts, then give the names: each name gets its own branch with the same steps, and a task in the epic of the plan, whose steps are those steps.

Nothing is copied: there is still one epic, one task and one page, and the plan only points at them.

### Archive a plan, or carry on

**Archive plan…** puts the plan away when it is all done, or carries the open parts over to a new plan (the old one keeps a line, “Moved to …”). An archived plan is listed under **Archived**, stays readable, and **Open it again** brings it back.

### In the file

A plan is a page like any other: the properties (`tags: [plan]`), a heading, a few lines if you like, and the parts as a list (`- part`, indented for a smaller part, with `[[links]]`). Nexmio Work only changes the list, and keeps everything else in the page as it was. A line that starts with `- [ ]` is a task, not a part.

### Arrange the map

A big map can get wide. Right-click a part that has parts under it and choose **Arrange the parts**: **side by side** (one row), **stacked** (under each other, with the line coming in from the side), or in 2 or 3 columns or rows. For any other number, choose the part and use the grid box in the panel on the right: write a number and press **Columns** or **Rows** (nine parts, 3 columns, is a 3 by 3 grid; by columns the order runs along each row, by rows it runs down each column). You can also drag a box to move it (the parts under it follow), drag the empty map to move around, and zoom with the mouse wheel (or the buttons), and press **Tidy up** to put every box back where the map places it. The arrangement is kept for each plan on this computer; the plan itself is not changed. A part that is tied to a task, a page or an epic shows small buttons for them under its name.
