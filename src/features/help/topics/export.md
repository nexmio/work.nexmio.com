## Share a page

Choose **File → Export…** (or right-click a page or folder and choose **Export…**).

- **HTML**: one file you can open in any browser. Pictures are included.
- **Word (.docx)**: for sending to people who use Word.
- **PDF**: opens a print preview. Choose _Save as PDF_ in the print dialog.

You can export one page or a whole folder. Exporting does not change your pages and makes no entry in the history.
