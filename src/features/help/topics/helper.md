## The taskbar helper

On **Windows**, Nexmio Work can sit in the taskbar and answer a key you press in **any program**. Two keys are made for it:

- **Write something down** (standard keys **Ctrl+Alt+N**): a small window opens over what you are doing. Choose **task** or **idea**, write one line and press Enter. A task goes into the epic you choose, an idea into Ideas. The window closes and you are back where you were.
- **Where I left off** (standard keys **Ctrl+Alt+P**): a small window with the task you are working on first. Write one line about the next step. It is written in the task's log as "Left off: …" and shows on the task page and on the start page the next time you come back.

Press **Esc** in the small window to close it without saving. It works on the vault that is open in Nexmio Work, so open one first.

### Turn it on and choose the keys

Open **Settings → Pages and backup → Taskbar helper**.

1. Tick **Turn on the taskbar helper**. It is off until you do.
2. Click the box under each function and **press the keys you want**, for example Ctrl+Alt+G. Use Ctrl, Alt or Shift together with a letter, a digit or an F key. **Use Ctrl+Alt+N** puts the standard keys back.
3. Press **Save**. Under each key it says **Works**, or why not: **Another program already uses these keys** means choose other keys here.

**Keep Nexmio Work running when I close the window** hides the window when you close it and leaves Nexmio Work in the taskbar, so the keys keep working. The icon's menu has **Open Nexmio Work**, **Write something down**, **Where I left off** and **Quit Nexmio Work**, which ends Nexmio Work for real. Click the icon to open the window.

### Only on Windows

The keys and the taskbar icon work on Windows only. On other systems the whole **Taskbar helper** part of the settings is greyed out and says so, because a key that works in every program cannot be trusted there. Nothing else in Nexmio Work depends on it.

The helper never sends anything anywhere: the small window is a part of Nexmio Work, and what you write goes into your own pages.
