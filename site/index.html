How it works

Write. Organise. Ask. Keep.

WRITE

Plain Markdown

A fast editor that shows your formatting as you type. Every page is an ordinary .md file in an ordinary folder.

ORGANISE

Tasks and meetings

Tasks live in your pages. Meetings have an agenda, a place to write while you talk, and the tasks that came out of them.

ASK

An AI that suggests

Fix, rewrite or find things with a model on your own computer. It proposes; you read the change and accept it.

KEEP

Nothing is lost

Every change is saved in a built-in Git history. Bring back any version of a page, or a page you deleted.

Features

Everything in one place, nothing locked in.

✓

Tasks that live in your pages

A task is a checklist line, so it works in any editor. Group them into epics, see one epic at a time, and focus on one thing at a time.

  • Epics with steps, a page and a log of what happened
  • A status on every task: Doing, Waiting or Dropped
  • A timer that writes the time in the task's log, and a Time view for invoices
✦

An AI that knows your work, and only suggests

Ask what to do next or what you are waiting for, or select text and choose what to do. Nothing is written until you accept it.

  • It reads your tasks, projects and meetings, and answers from them
  • It can suggest tasks and parts of a plan, for you to accept
  • Runs on your computer: built-in models, or LM Studio
  • Fixed commands that do one thing and return only the result
◧

Pages for the whole meeting

Write your pages in one place all through the meeting. Add only the parts you want: an agenda, decisions, and actions that become tasks.

  • An action is a task in your page, so it shows in Tasks too
  • A small arrow writes an agenda point into the pages
▦

Projects, one part at a time

Break something big into parts, and the parts into smaller parts. See it as a map or as an outline, and tie a part to a task or a page.

  • An overview of each project: how far it has come, what to do now, what waits, its meetings and decisions
  • Arrange the map: side by side, stacked or in a grid
  • Archive a plan when it is done, and open it again if you need it
↺

Look back on your week

See what you finished, the time you logged, the meetings you held and the pages you wrote, all in one place, week by week.

  • Copy it as a status report, or let the AI write a few sentences from it
  • One project or all of them
☰

A start page and your own look

Your start page shows what you need today, built from widgets you choose. Pick Light, Dark or one of six calm palettes.

  • Next meetings, recent pages, plans and epic progress at a glance
  • Your pages never change: the theme is only how the app looks
#

Your own tags, kept apart

Tag a page with any word and find it again in search. The tags the app uses are shown apart, with what each one does.

  • Tags are written in the page's properties, so any editor reads them
  • The AI never adds or takes away the app's own tags

Your files

A folder of Markdown, and nothing else.

Open your vault in any other editor, any day. There is no database to export and no format to escape from.

Plain files, by design

What you write lives as ordinary files on your disk.

  • No database to export
  • Properties on top of the page, so any editor reads them
  • Every change kept in Git history
  • It all still opens, with or without Nexmio Work

Privacy

Your pages stay on your computer.

No account, no tracking

Nexmio Work does not phone home. There is nothing to sign up for.

A local AI by default

The AI reads your pages only on your own machine. A cloud AI is possible, but only after you say yes, and never in a project you lock to local.

You decide what is written

The AI never changes a page on its own. Every change is a proposal you review.
